# Contributing to GoalHyke

First off, thank you for considering contributing to GoalHyke! It's people like you that make GoalHyke such a great tool.

## Branch Naming Conventions

To keep our branch structure clean and organized, please follow these naming conventions:

-   **feature/\<branch-name>**: For new features. (e.g., `feature/user-authentication`)
-   **bugfix/\<branch-name>**: For fixing bugs. (e.g., `bugfix/login-form-validation`)
-   **chore/\<branch-name>**: For maintenance tasks, refactoring, and other non-feature changes. (e.g., `chore/update-dependencies`)
-   **docs/\<branch-name>**: For documentation changes. (e.g., `docs/add-contributing-guide`)

## Pull Request (PR) Process

1.  **Fork the repository** and create your branch from `main`.
2.  **Make your changes** in your forked repository.
3.  **Ensure your code lints** and follows the project's code style.
4.  **Write tests** for your changes.
5.  **Push your changes** to your fork.
6.  **Open a pull request** to the `main` branch of the main repository.
7.  **Title your PR** clearly and concisely.
8.  **Provide a detailed description** of the changes in your PR.
9.  **Link any relevant issues** in your PR description.
10. **Request a review** from one or more of the project maintainers.
11. **Respond to feedback** and make any necessary changes.

Once your PR is approved, a maintainer will merge it into the `main` branch.
